# Design — SpinCoatSim

## Shared contract (normative)

This application consumes `@jorpago2/scientific-ui` and follows the [shared interface contract](https://github.com/jorpago2/jorpago2.github.io/blob/main/docs/interface-contract.md).

## Scientific exceptions

- Material and film colours identify stack layers and are not application accents.
- The cross-section canvas and coating metrics remain product-specific.
- Reference recipes must distinguish published values, edits, defaults and custom calibration without relying on colour alone.
- Automatic recalculation must expose freshness and the most recent update time.
